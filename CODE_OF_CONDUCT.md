# Amahame y'imyitwarire — Code of Conduct

## Isezerano ryacu — Our pledge

Twiyemeje gutuma kugira uruhare muri Wandaa ari ibintu bitagira ivangura ku
muntu wese, hatitawe ku myaka, ubumuga, ubwoko, igitsina, uburambe,
uburezi, ubukungu, ubwenegihugu, isura, idini, cyangwa ururimi avuga.

We pledge to make participation in Wandaa a harassment-free experience for
everyone, regardless of age, disability, ethnicity, gender, level of
experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, appearance,
religion, or the language they speak.

## Ibyiza — Expected behaviour

- Kubaha abandi n'ibitekerezo byabo.
- Kwakira inama nziza n'ubugororangingo.
- Kwibanda ku cyiza cya bose, atari icyawe wenyine.
- Kwihanganira abatangiye — Wandaa igenewe abiga.

Wandaa exists partly so that people can learn to program in their own language.
Treat beginners' questions as the point of the project, not an interruption.

**Language.** Issues, pull requests and reviews are welcome in **Kinyarwanda,
English or French**. Nobody is required to switch languages to be taken
seriously. If you cannot read a contribution, ask for a translation rather than
dismissing it. Imperfect English is not a reason to discount someone's
technical argument.

## Ibibujijwe — Unacceptable behaviour

- Amagambo y'urukozasoni cyangwa ashingiye ku gitsina.
- Gutuka, gutoteza, cyangwa kugaba ibitero ku muntu ku giti cye.
- Gushyira ahagaragara amakuru bwite y'undi utabiherewe uburenganzira.
- Kwirengagiza no gusuzugura abatangiye kubera ko batazi.
- Kwigana cyangwa kwiyitirira umuntu cyangwa ikigo.

Sexualised language or imagery; insults, harassment, or personal attacks;
publishing others' private information without permission; dismissing or
belittling beginners; impersonating a person or organisation.

## Aho bikurikizwa — Scope

This applies in all project spaces — the repository, issues, pull requests,
discussions — and when representing the project publicly.

## Gutanga ikirego — Reporting

Report conduct problems to **conduct@vafubwengetech.rw**, or privately to any
maintainer listed in [MAINTAINERS.md](MAINTAINERS.md).

Reports are handled confidentially. You will get an acknowledgement within
**3 working days**. If your report concerns a maintainer, you may send it to
any other maintainer, who will handle it without involving the subject.

## Ingaruka — Enforcement

Maintainers will use their judgement, proportionate to the behaviour:

1. **Kuburira** — a private warning explaining what was wrong.
2. **Kwihanangiriza** — a public warning, and a period with no interaction on
   the project.
3. **Guhagarika by'igihe gito** — a temporary ban.
4. **Guhagarika burundu** — a permanent ban.

Decisions and reasoning are recorded privately by the maintainers. A person
subject to enforcement may appeal once, in writing, to the maintainers as a
group.
