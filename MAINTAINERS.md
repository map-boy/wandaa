## Areas looking for a maintainer

These are real gaps, not busywork. Each is scoped in [ROADMAP.md](ROADMAP.md).

- **Standard library** — `lib/*.waa`, and the missing modules (JSON, dates)
- **Package manager** — `wandaa` CLI, manifest and lockfile format, registry
- **Databases** — SQLite and Postgres bindings over FFI
- **Web and networking** — Winsock bindings, HTTP server
- **Numerics and ML** — floating point in the compiler, then tensors
- **Editor tooling** — language server, formatter, debugger
- **Documentation and translation** — especially Kinyarwanda-first teaching material

If you want one of these, open an issue saying so. You do not need permission to
start; you need it to be merged.
